05FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Webadi do?

Webadi is a web design studio that designs and builds custom websites, from the first design to launch on the client’s own domain, including animation, mobile-first layouts and features like reservations, applications and donations.

Who does Webadi build websites for?

Small businesses and nonprofits, including restaurants and hospitality businesses, nonprofits and foundations, and boutique service businesses.

Where is Webadi based?

Webadi is based in New York City and Jackson Hole, Wyoming, and works with clients wherever they are.

Are Webadi websites mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every Webadi website is designed mobile-first, so it looks and works great on phones, tablets and computers.

Do you use templates?

No. Every website is custom-designed around the client’s brand and what their business needs most.

How do I start a project with Webadi?

Send a message to @webadi.bb on Instagram with a few details about your business and what you need.
